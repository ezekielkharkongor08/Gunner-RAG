Arsenal had a busy March, playing several matches. They started the month with a 2-1 win over Chelsea at home, with goals from Saliba and Timber [1]. They then played Brighton at the Amex Stadium on 4 March, winning 1-0 with a goal from Saka [3]. On 7 March, they traveled to Mansfield Town for the FA Cup 5th Round, winning 2-1 with goals from Madueke and Eze [4]. They also played Bayer Leverkusen in the Champions League round of 16, drawing 1-1 in the first leg [5] and winning 3-1 in the second leg [10]. On 14 March, they beat Everton 2-0 at home, with goals from Gyökeres and Dowman [7]. On 17 March, they beat Bayer Leverkusen 3-1 in the Champions League round of 16 second leg [10]. On 22 March, they lost 2-0 to Manchester City in the EFL Cup final [12].
